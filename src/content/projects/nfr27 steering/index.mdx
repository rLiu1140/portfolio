### Overview

Formula SAE requires all teams to develop a mechanical steering system. Previously, Northwestern Formula Racing has solely used 4130 chromoly steel components to achieve a robust, high-stiffness system.

I am independently working on a new steering system for the 2027 car, exploring alternative materials and structural concepts to reduce overall weight.
