### Overview
To commemorate FRC Team 610's 25th anniversary, I designed and manufactured custom plaques to recognize mentors and individuals who have continuously supported the team.

### Design
The plaque featured the Team 610 logo with recessed pockets to highlight negative space. It was finished with an adhesive felt layer on the back.

### Finishing
To take advantage of CNC milling, I selected a crosshatched toolpath finish. An initial trial used small stepovers to create a fine pattern, but this resulted in excessive machining time. Increasing the stepover produced a larger hatch pattern while significantly reducing the machining time.

### Manufacturing
The initial design featured a small logo that required 1/16-in-diameter end mills for the recessed features. These tools were fragile, costly, and had a slow feed rate, which increased complexity and machining time. Marginally increasing the logo size allowed for the use of a stronger 3/32-in end mill instead.

Maintaining workpiece flatness during machining was also crucial for a consistent crosshatched pattern. Secure workholding was achieved with multiple clamps.
