### Overview
To commemorate FRC Team 610's 25th anniversary, I manufactured a small batch of custom plaques to recognize mentors and individuals who have continuously supported the team.

### Design
The plaque featured the Team 610 logo with recessed pockets to highlight negative space. An adhesive-backed felt layer was applied to the rear of each plaque.

### Finishing
Taking advantage of CNC milling, a crosshatched toolpath finish was selected. Initial trials used small stepovers to create a fine pattern, but this resulted in excessive machining time. Increasing the stepover produced a larger hatch pattern while significantly reducing machining time.

### Manufacturing
The initial design featured a smaller logo that required 1/16" diameter end mills for the recessed features. These tools were costly and fragile, and their low feed rates increased machining time. By marginally increasing the logo size, a stronger 3/32" end mill could be used.

Maintaining workpiece flatness during machining was also crucial for a consistent crosshatched pattern, which was achieved through secure workholding with multiple clamps.
